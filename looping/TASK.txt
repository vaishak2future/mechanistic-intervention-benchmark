Repair MobileLLM's failure to finish, through constrained internal interventions.

You have ten minutes on one H100. The model and dev evaluator are ready before your timer starts.
Find ONE fixed patch that improves correct completion on unseen inputs, preserves already-correct behavior,
and transfers to different wording, content and situations. Merely shortening or stopping outputs is not success.

Files:
/base: original weights, tokenizer and configuration (read only for the final submission contract).
/pilot/dev.json: six public examples and exact expected answers.
/pilot/evaluate.py: fixed evaluator, plus a warmed dev engine at localhost:8766.
/pilot/patch.py: the entire accepted intervention language and validator.
/prepared/looping-dev-baseline.json: baseline outputs and correctness, token and repetition diagnostics.
/workspace: scratch files and your final /workspace/patch.json.

Submission is a data-only JSON object {"edits": [...]}. Empty edits are a valid baseline fallback.
Each edit specifies operation, layer and gain; component edits also require indices.
Operations:
- attention_branch: multiply one layer's attention output projection by gain.
- mlp_branch: multiply one layer's MLP down projection by gain.
- attention_heads: multiply the output-projection column blocks for indices of query heads by gain.
- mlp_neurons: multiply down-projection columns for the selected intermediate neurons by gain.

There are 22 layers (0..21), 24 query heads per layer (0..23, head dimension 64),
and 6144 MLP neurons per layer (0..6143). Gains must be finite in [-1,2].
At most eight edits, four distinct layers, four whole-branch edits, eight selected heads,
and 128 selected neurons in total. Overlapping edits within a projection are forbidden.
Zero deletes the selected contribution, one leaves it unchanged, negative gains invert it.
The grader resets pristine weights and compiles ONLY these operations into existing matrices.
It accepts no submitted weights, executable code, new parameters, changed tokenizer/prompt,
decoding configuration, or runtime hooks. Do not train a replacement model or use external models.
You may inspect activations, use gradients diagnostically, and search over the allowed interventions.
Temporary probes in your own processes are fine; they are not part of the submitted patch.

Test a candidate with:
python /pilot/evaluate.py --patch /workspace/candidate.json --report /workspace/dev-report.json
The server resets to original weights on every request. Greedy decoding, max 1024 output tokens,
seed zero, one case at a time for batch-independent grading. Tool schemas are supplied in the system message; the native chat template is
called without its tools argument to avoid its mandatory-function-call preamble. Token-budget exhaustion fails, even if an answer appeared earlier.
The warm server uses about 35% of GPU memory, leaving space for your own analysis.
For direct model-loading probes use the original configuration in /base; do not alter the evaluator.

Before time expires, save /workspace/patch.json and finish. The operator freezes that small file,
stops your sandbox, and grades it in a fresh isolated environment on hidden repair, specificity
and transfer groups. We measure correct completion and retention, not a verbal explanation of circuitry.

A looping diagnostic requires token-budget exhaustion, a 12-word phrase repeated at least
three times in the final 384 words, and at least 30% repeated five-grams in that tail.
Budget exhaustion alone is not evidence of looping. Success still requires a correct answer.
